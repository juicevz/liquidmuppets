LIQUIDMUPPETS OFFICIAL UPDATE
12 September 2026 · Stock Drops

Copy below each label. Implementation announcement; mainnet deployment and first funding are pending.

[RECOMMENDED UPDATE]

Introducing Muppet Stock Drops.

Each 15,000 $MUPPETS held at the snapshot gives one allocation unit. Funded stock tokens are split proportionally and claimed to your wallet.

Code and tests published. First mainnet drop pending.

https://github.com/juicevz/liquidmuppets

[STANDALONE 2: DIRECT CLAIMS]

Stock Drops adds a direct stock-token claim for eligible $MUPPETS holders.

No Agent Key, token deposit or lock is required. Each drop must hold its entire published budget before claims exist.

The implementation is published; mainnet activation is pending.

[STANDALONE 3: ALLOCATION EVIDENCE]

Every Stock Drop publishes its snapshot, excluded wallets, allocation amounts and claim proofs.

The claim page checks that file against the funded contract before enabling a transaction.

Code is published. The first mainnet snapshot and funding are pending.

[STANDALONE 4: IMMUTABLE DROPS]

Funded Stock Drop allocations cannot be replaced or redirected by the publisher. Claims have no expiry and always pay the committed recipient.

The contract and wallet flow are implemented and tested. Mainnet deployment and the first funded drop remain pending.

[STANDALONE 5: ASSET SUPPORT]

Stock Drops initially supports AAPL, AMD, AMZN and ASML Stock Tokens, with one asset per funded drop.

An enabled asset does not mean a funded reward. Each pot needs a separately approved budget.

Implementation published. First mainnet funding pending.

[THREAD 1/7]

1/7

Muppet Stock Drops is a new holder feature in the LiquidMuppets repository.

It combines a fully funded stock-token distributor, historical balance snapshots, public allocations and a wallet claim page.

Mainnet deployment and the first pot are pending.

[THREAD 2/7]

2/7

Eligibility uses wallet balances at a published block. Each full 15,000 $MUPPETS gives one unit.

The stock-token budget is divided proportionally across eligible units. Integer remainders are allocated deterministically so every funded raw token unit is assigned.

[THREAD 3/7]

3/7

The publisher must deposit the entire reward budget when committing a drop.

There is no automatic fee feed, fixed payout schedule or promised return. Existing vault deposits and FeeRwaReserve inventory remain separate from holder allocations.

[THREAD 4/7]

4/7

The public allocation file contains the full holder snapshot and excluded wallets with reasons.

Publisher tooling checks historical balances against archive RPC. The contract enforces the committed allocation; it does not verify past token balances itself.

[THREAD 5/7]

5/7

Claiming sends the allocated stock tokens directly to the committed wallet.

No Key, MUPPETS deposit or approval is required. Holders pay network gas in ETH.

Claims have no expiry. Issuer transfer restrictions can still affect execution.

[THREAD 6/7]

6/7

Validation covers funding, proofs, replay protection, duplicate claims, exact transfers and wallet/network changes.

The complete browser flow passed on a local chain using synthetic assets. A separate mainnet-fork test passed for all four supported stock tokens.

[THREAD 7/7]

7/7

Remaining launch steps: independent review, verified Safe deployment, snapshot publication, an approved funded pot and the production release.

Stock Drops operates independently of FactoryV2 and the pending Agent Bond activation.
